Frequently asked questions

What is xSave?

xSave is a free Chrome extension for downloading videos from X (formerly Twitter). It adds Save video, Copy link, and Post video actions directly to supported video posts.

How do I save a video from X?

Add xSave to Chrome, open or refresh x.com, and find a video post. Click Save video in the xSave controls. For a quoted video or full-screen player, use Open post to save video first.

Is it really free?

Yes. xSave is free to install and use. You don’t need to create an xSave account or visit an ad-filled downloader website.

Does it work with Twitter links?

Yes. xSave supports x.com and twitter.com. Open the video’s post in Chrome with the extension enabled.

What if a download doesn’t work?

Refresh X, open the original post, and make sure xSave is enabled and up to date. Some videos may not be accessible. If you need a hand, visit xSave support for troubleshooting and contact details.

Can I reuse a downloaded video?

Downloading doesn’t transfer ownership. Respect the creator’s rights, get permission when needed, and follow the platform’s terms when sharing someone else’s work.
