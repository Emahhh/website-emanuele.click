About

Hi! I'm Emanuele. I'm currently a Computer Science Master's student at University of Pisa. I have a passion for technology and building things that make life easier. I won the Apple Swift Student Challenge with one of my iOS apps, but I also love full-stack development, AI and learning new technologies. I'm fluent in Italian and English.

Projects

Below are just some of my personal projects:

ShowerTime – A fun and educational iOS app that helps users to save water. Winner of the Apple Swift Student Challenge. Built with Swift and SwiftUI.
Persona Genius – An AI-powered collaborative web app to help you create user personas. Built with JavaScript, Svelte, Firebase, and Node.js.
OnePic – A React app that lets you create beautiful photo collages with ease. Upload your pictures and arrange them into stunning layouts. Built with React.
Marginate – An iOS app that helps you to add margins to your PDFs. Built with Swift and React.
mailtoFixer.js – A JS script that fixes mailto links in any web page by making them more usable.
EyeCare – An iOS app that helps users to take care of their eyes. It features live camera filters, face tracking features, speech recognition and much more. Built with Swift, SwiftUI, ARKit and RealityKit.
Magnishort – A web app that converts Magnet URLs into short, shareable links. Built with PHP + SQLite.
