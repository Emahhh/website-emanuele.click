Privacy policy

Questions

Is StickersBase free?

Yes. Making stickers, adding them to WhatsApp and browsing Discover are free, with no account.

Which posts can I turn into stickers?

Public X posts with a video, GIF or photo. Private, deleted or age-restricted posts can’t be imported. You can always use videos, GIFs and photos from your camera roll.

Are the stickers animated?

Yes. Every sticker is an animated WhatsApp sticker, up to 3 seconds. Still photos work too.

Does it work with Telegram or iMessage?

StickersBase is made for WhatsApp. You can also share the original video or save it to Photos.

Can I share any sticker with the community?

Only stickers you have the right to share. A public post isn’t automatically free to redistribute. Read the community rules.

How do I report a sticker or request a takedown?

In the app, open the sticker and tap ⋯ → Report. Rights holders can email emanuele.click.dev+stickersbase@gmail.com.
