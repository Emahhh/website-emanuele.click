Questions

Is StickersBase free?

Yes. You can make stickers and add them to WhatsApp for free, with no account.

What can I turn into a sticker?

Videos, GIFs and photos from your camera roll, or from public X posts. Only use content you have the right to use.

Are the stickers animated?

Yes. Still photos work too.

Something’s wrong with a sticker I found.

Open it in the app and tap Report, or email us.
